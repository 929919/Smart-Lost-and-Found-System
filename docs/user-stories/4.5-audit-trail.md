## Story
> As an **administrator**, I want a record of who logged or changed each item so that decisions can be reviewed later.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Changes to items and claims are recorded with the actor and timestamp
- [ ] The history of a record can be reviewed
- [ ] History entries cannot be edited or removed

## Implementation
**Not delivered.** The lowest-priority story in the backlog. Partially mitigated: `claims` records `created_at` and `updated_at`, so claim decisions carry timestamps, and row-level security already prevents records being deleted from the client. A complete trail requires a dedicated table recording actor, action and target.
